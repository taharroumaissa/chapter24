Looking Back

My Journey

13 June 2002

The Beginning

Arrived on a Thursday in June. Nobody warned the world. Nobody asked if I was ready. I wasn't — but here we are, twenty-four years later.

2007

First Day of School

Walked in with a backpack bigger than me and immediately started pretending to understand what the teacher was saying. A skill I still use today.

2019

Baccalaureate — Year 1

Passed the first Bac. Survived the most stressful exam Algeria has to offer and came out the other side with a certificate and a newfound appreciation for summer.

2020

Baccalaureate — Year 2

Second Bac, done. The world locked down. I did not. Between pandemic silence and study sessions, the idea of building things with code started feeling less like a plan and more like a calling.

2021

Baccalaureate — Year 3

Third year, third Bac. By now the grind was familiar. Discipline over motivation. Showed up anyway. Passed anyway. That lesson turned out to be more valuable than the diploma itself.

2022

Baccalaureate — Year 4 + University Begins

Fourth Bac. Then, in September 2022, something shifted: first lecture on algorithms. A professor wrote pseudocode on a board and something clicked that has never unclicked since. This was the language I had been looking for.

Now

Master 1 — Software Engineering

Living the dream specialty. Building StoreNest on the side. Twenty-four years old, a Master's in progress, and a whole future left to write — in clean, well-commented code.
